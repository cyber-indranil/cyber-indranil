## About Me

I am a cybercrime investigator, cybersecurity researcher and technology builder focused on developing practical systems for lawful investigation, digital forensics, public safety and capacity-building within the law-enforcement ecosystem.

My work combines investigative experience with software development to transform real operational requirements into dependable Android, web and server-based platforms.

- Cyber Ambassador — **ISEA, C-DAC, MeitY**
- Cybercrime investigation and digital evidence analysis
- OSINT, malware intelligence and forensic research
- Android, PHP–MySQL and Python-based tool development
- Cybersecurity training and knowledge sharing for LEA personnel
- Privacy-conscious and evidence-oriented system design
